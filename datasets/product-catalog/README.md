# Product Catalog Dataset

Product master table for product-level commercial analysis.

- 10 products
- Grain: one row per product
- Primary key: `product_id`
- Intended use: product mix, list-price comparison, standard-margin benchmarking, and join practice

## Relationship

Product names in the catalog are represented in the `orders.product_name` field, allowing learners to practice product-level joins and benchmark actual order economics against product master attributes.
